Second Glance is a photography project exploring the beauty of everyday moments that often go unnoticed.

The goal is to encourage viewers to slow down, observe their surroundings, and discover new perspectives through photography.

The collection explores everyday environments, architecture, colors, patterns, and spontaneous moments.
